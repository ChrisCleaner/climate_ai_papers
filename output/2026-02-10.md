# Research Summary: Decision-Oriented AI Weather Forecasting for Indian Monsoon

**Main Contribution:**
Introduces a decision-oriented benchmarking framework that evaluates AI weather prediction (AIWP) models based on stakeholder needs rather than standard meteorological metrics. Demonstrates application to Indian monsoon forecasting with direct deployment to 38 million farmers in 2025.

**Method:**
Integrates meteorology, AI, and social science perspectives to assess AIWP model performance using both deterministic and probabilistic metrics. Focuses on agriculturally relevant indices (monsoon onset) evaluated out-of-sample at regional scales with multi-week lead times.

**Data:**
150 years of Indian monsoon historical records; evaluation using open-access AIWP models; operational forecasts deployed through government channels.

**Climate Relevance:**
Addresses monsoon prediction—a critical climate phenomenon affecting rain-fed agriculture in South Asia. Directly supports climate adaptation for populations vulnerable to monsoon variability and climate change impacts on precipitation patterns.

**Implications:**
Demonstrates scalable pathway for translating AI forecasting advances into actionable guidance for vulnerable populations. Successfully captured anomalous monsoon behavior (weeks-long pause), validating operational utility beyond standard benchmarks.

**Limitations:**
Framework application limited to single case study (Indian monsoon); generalizability to other regions/decision contexts not yet demonstrated. Stakeholder engagement depth and long-term outcome tracking not detailed.

**TL;DR:**
Decision-focused evaluation of AI weather models enables monsoon forecasts that directly benefit 38 million Indian farmers, bridging AI capability gaps with climate adaptation needs.
