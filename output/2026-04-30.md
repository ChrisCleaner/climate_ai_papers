# Research Summary: Elastic-Viscous-Plastic Sea-Ice Model with Kelvin-Voigt Rheology

**Method:**
- Introduces Voigt regularization in momentum equations rather than constitutive relations
- Proves local well-posedness with advection; global well-posedness without advection
- Derives novel L∞-estimates for stress tensors exploiting damping structure
- Handles unbounded viscosity coefficients (resolves limitation in related Hibler model)

**Data:**
No empirical datasets used; purely theoretical mathematical analysis.

**Climate Relevance:**

**Implications:**
Provides rigorous mathematical foundation for improved sea-ice constitutive models, enabling more stable and accurate numerical implementations in coupled climate models without artificial viscosity cutoffs.

**Limitations:**
