This paper critically evaluates Ditlevsen & Ditlevsen (2023)'s statistical framework for predicting Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation (AMOC) collapse timing. The authors demonstrate that the original study's confidence interval (2037–2109) is unreliably narrow due to insufficient uncertainty quantification.

**Method:**

**Climate Relevance:**

**Implications:**

**Limitations:**

**TL;DR:**
