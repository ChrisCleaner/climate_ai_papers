# Research Summary: Higher-Order Time Integration for Cloud Microphysics

**Implications:**

**Limitations:**
Analysis limited to P3 scheme in E3SMv3; generalizability to other microphysics schemes or models not explicitly addressed; practical implementation in production models requires further validation.

**TL;DR:**

# Research Summary: Data-Driven Integration Kernels for Nonlocal Operator Learning

**Main Contribution:**
Introduces data-driven integration kernels that structure nonlocal operator learning by separating spatial-temporal information aggregation from local nonlinear prediction. This approach improves interpretability and reduces overfitting while maintaining predictive performance.

**Method:**

- Aggregated features feed into a local nonlinear mapping (neural network)
- Hierarchical model progression: baseline → nonparametric kernels → parametric kernels
- Kernels directly reveal which spatial locations, vertical levels, and past timesteps contribute to predictions

**Data:**
South Asian monsoon precipitation system; specific datasets not detailed in abstract.

**Climate Relevance:**
Addresses a critical challenge in climate ML: capturing nonlocal atmospheric processes (e.g., moisture transport, convective organization, teleconnections) that drive regional precipitation while maintaining physical interpretability.

**Implications:**

**Limitations:**
