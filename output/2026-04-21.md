- Includes downscaling to U.S. coastal regions

**Data:**
EPA vehicle emissions inventories; SSP2-4.5 baseline scenario; alternative scenarios (stalled technology, pre-regulation fleet counterfactual).

**Climate Relevance:**
Quantifies transportation sector's contribution to global mean sea-level rise and regional coastal impacts—critical for understanding sectoral climate mitigation priorities and coastal adaptation planning.

**Implications:**

**Limitations:**
Apparent reliance on single climate-sea-level model pairing; uncertainty in long-term ice sheet dynamics (2100-2200) remains substantial; regional downscaling methodology not detailed.

**TL;DR:**
