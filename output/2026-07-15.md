**Climate Relevance:**
Ocean heat content is the primary metric for monitoring Earth Energy Imbalance and climate system energy accumulation; reliable uncertainty estimates are essential for detecting statistically significant climate trends.

**Implications:**
Enables more rigorous regional and global OHC anomaly analysis with quantified confidence intervals, improving detection of climate signals and validation of climate models.

**Limitations:**
Analysis restricted to two pressure layers; applicability to deeper ocean sections or multi-layer systems not addressed; temporal scope limited to 2004–2022 (post-Argo era).

**TL;DR:**
Joint statistical modeling of correlated ocean layers reduces OHC uncertainty by 15%, enabling more reliable climate monitoring.
